Yeungnam University Startup Support Foundation Holds the 2nd YUnicorn ESG Startup Competition 2024

Sep 2, 2024 · Source: Yeungnam University Public Relations

StegaVision won the Grand Prize in the pre-founder track at the 2nd YUnicorn ESG Startup Competition, held on August 29, 2024 at the Chunma Art Center, Yeungnam University.

StegaVision presented a DFC-based digital identity and payment system. The competition was co-hosted by Gyeongsan City and Gyeongsangbuk-do and organized by the Yeungnam University Startup Support Foundation.

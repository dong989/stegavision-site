OUR BUSINESS

What is AI-based Invisible Steganography authentication?

An AI Encoder embeds data into the original image. The image looks the same to the naked eye,
while an AI Decoder can decode the embedded information.

Original quality preserved (invisibility)

Applying the AI Encoder does not change the resolution or color of the image,
so to the naked eye it keeps the same quality as the original.

Protection against forgery and tampering

The embedded information is encrypted in the pixel patterns of the image, making copying or
tampering practically impossible and enabling tracking and authenticity checks.

Works across many media

It applies to images, video, printed materials, displays and more,
making it highly scalable as security infrastructure across industries.

High recovery rate

The AI Decoder precisely decodes the embedded information,
with an excellent data recovery rate even under loss or distortion.
